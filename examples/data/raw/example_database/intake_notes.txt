Intake Station Notes

The intake station scans each incoming widget's barcode and records its lot
number. Lots that fail the visual inspection are diverted to the rework bin.
Typical intake rate is 130 units per hour, slightly above the line's 120 u/h
throughput, so a small buffer accumulates before calibration. The buffer is
capped at 25 units; beyond that, intake pauses until calibration catches up.
